# Inside Addictive Design: Evidence From Internal Corporate Documents

An interactive research dashboard exploring what internal corporate documents reveal about technology companies' understanding, investigation, and potential use of product design mechanisms associated with habitual, compulsive, or excessive use.

**Live dashboard:** https://leahlashaehill.github.io/inside-addictive-design/

## What's inside
- **Research Overview** – key metrics, evidence ladder, recurring themes, strongest findings, charts
- **Document Explorer** – every document with its findings, quotations, locations and limitations
- **Evidence Explorer** – individual findings with search, filters and CSV export
- **Historical Timeline** – documents by recorded document date (precision preserved)
- **Themes & Patterns**, **Knowledge vs. Intent**, **Research Connections** (network + relationship table)
- **Findings Summary** and **Methodology & Validation**

## How to read it
- Quotations marked **Verbatim** are exact text from source documents as recorded in the dataset.
- **CSV annotation** notes come from an AI-assisted document review.
- **Dashboard synthesis** (themes, classifications, summaries) is AI-generated interpretation and should be verified against the sources.
- Relevance levels describe research importance, not proof of addiction or wrongdoing. Awareness is not treated as proof of intentional addictive design, and the dataset does not establish causation.
- The dataset is a subset of a larger archive. Links to original documents may require access permission.

## Viewing locally
Download `index.html` and open it in any browser. It is fully self-contained and works offline.
